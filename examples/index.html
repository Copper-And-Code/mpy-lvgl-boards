    Examples¶

    They run on every board of this repo (the RP2350-Touch-LCD-4 at 240x240, the STM32H743 boards at their panel's size, 1024x600 on the IPS panel) with the same code.

    RP2350: run one with mpremote run examples/<name>.py; to start it at power-on copy it to the board as main.py (mpremote cp examples/<name>.py :main.py). STM32H7_CORE V1.3 on the bench (CH340 on UART1, DTR/RTS wired to BOOT0/NRST): python3 scripts/uart_run.py examples/<name>.py (see docs/stm32h743.md).

    Example Shows
    hello.py the minimum: import display_driver, a label, a spinner
    button.py touch: a button counting taps
    animation.py LVGL animations (no tearing: double buffered)
    backlight.py a slider setting the backlight with display_driver.backlight() (on/off on the STM32 boards for now)
    c64_keyboard.py typing on a C64-style keyboard (TCA8418 on connector H4 of the RP2350 board, see python/c64keyboard.py); without it, says so
    breakout.py a game: drag the paddle, break the bricks (ball physics in an LVGL timer, buzzer if the board has one)
    pong.py a game: you against the microcontroller, drag your paddle up and down
    simon.py a game: repeat the growing sequence of colours (LVGL timer, buzzer if the board has one)
    snake.py a game: snake controlled by tapping on the side to turn to (LVGL objects reused as segments)
    tictactoe.py a game: you (X) against the microcontroller (O, minimax: it never loses)
    shell/ a Linux-like shell on the screen, keyboard from a terminal on the serial port: files, pipes, wildcards, history, Tab; with vi and a friendlier edit, and an ANSI terminal module for your own programs. The font in ansifont.py is under the SIL OFL. Goes in / (shell.py, ansiterm.py, ansifont.py) and /bin (vi.py, edit.py), then import shell; shell.main(). On the RP2350 (no PSRAM) copy shell, ansiterm and ansifont compiled with mpy-cross (.mpy): compiled on the board they leave too little heap for the terminal's 115 KB framebuffer (MemoryError)
    weather.py ESP32-4848S040 only: weather, wind and 5 days of forecast from Open-Meteo, with drawn icons, setup on the touchscreen and settings on the SD card
    home/ ESP32-4848S040 only: the shell at power-on and a touch menu of apps (weather, games, Copper and Code Studio apps), each started alone after a reset; everything on the SD card but main.py

    Any resolution: sizes are written for a 240 px display and scaled with ui.px(n) (python/ui.py: n times the shorter side / 240, so unchanged on the RP2350 board), fonts with ui.font(size) (the closest Montserrat compiled in: 14, 16, 24, 32, 40), positions from display_driver.WIDTH / HEIGHT or LVGL alignment. Game speeds scale with ui.UNIT too; the breakout wall and the snake field get more columns on a wide display. Sound goes through python/buzzer.py (GPIO1 on the RP2350 board; silent where there is no buzzer, as on the STM32 boards so far).

    Every example ends after building its screen: display_driver keeps LVGL running from a timer. mpremote (or, on the STM32 bench, opening the serial port) resets the board before each run, so running another example replaces the previous one. Ctrl-C works as usual in programs with their own loop.
