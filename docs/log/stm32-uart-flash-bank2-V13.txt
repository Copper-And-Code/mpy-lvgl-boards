# STM32H7_CORE V1.3 (STM32H743, ROM bootloader v3.1, chip id 0x450), 2026-11-10: flashing
# STM32H7_CORE-V13-v0.1.5.hex (1 201 236 bytes, sectors 0-9: 0-7 in bank 1, 8-9 in bank 2)
# with scripts/stm32_uart_flash.py over the CH340 on UART1, 115200 baud.
#
# What happens: the bootloader ACKs the extended erase (0x44) once bank 1 is erased
# (6.3 s for sectors 0-7), then erases the bank 2 sectors with nothing on the line.
# Erasing sectors 8 and 9 alone, the ACK comes at once (0.0 s). Until it is done:
#  - a command gets no answer (GET polled every 0.8 s for 32 s after the 10-sector erase:
#    no byte back; sending them may also keep it from finishing, not checked);
#  - a reset (BOOT0 + NRST, to enter it again) stops the erase half way: afterwards
#    the start of sector 8 (0x08100000 to at least 0x08100a00) read as zeros, writes there
#    are ACKed but do not stick, reads further on in bank 2 can hang the bootloader
#    (probably flash ECC errors).
# Waiting in silence works: after erasing 8 and 9 the bootloader answers GET at 5 s and
# both sectors read 0xFF. Earlier images (up to 9 sectors, all in bank 1) never hit this;
# all four STM32 .hex files of release v0.1.5 reach sector 9.
# The fix (scripts/stm32_uart_flash.py, after the erase): with sectors of bank 2 in the
# image, wait 5, 10, 20, 40 s in silence and send GET until it answers.
# A board left half erased by the old script: flash again with the fixed one (it erases
# all the image's sectors again); attempt 4 below ran on such a board.
